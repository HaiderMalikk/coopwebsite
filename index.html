About Me

I am a third-year Software Engineering student at York University, specializing in Big Data. I am interested in how software and data can work together to solve practical problems, and I enjoy turning what I learn in the classroom into applications people can use.

Through my studies, personal projects, and co-op experience, I am developing my technical skills alongside the communication, collaboration, and professional judgment needed to work on a software development team.

What I Love About My Program

What I love most about my program is the opportunity to complete co-op terms. Co-op allows me to connect the concepts I study at York with real software development, learn from experienced professionals, and understand how teams approach problems beyond the classroom.

Each work term gives me a chance to explore my interests, take on new responsibilities, and return to my studies with a clearer understanding of why the material matters.

My Most Rewarding Co-op Experience

My most rewarding co-op experience so far was at RBC. It gave me a foundation in professionalism and helped me understand what software development looks like in a real workplace, where collaboration, communication, and accountability matter just as much as writing code.

I learned the importance of asking thoughtful questions, responding to feedback, and considering how my work fits into a larger team. Those lessons have stayed with me in my current co-op at D2L, and I will carry them throughout my career.

Career Goal

My goal is to become a software engineer who builds reliable, useful applications, with a focus on data-driven and AI-enabled systems. I want to combine my Big Data specialization with hands-on development experience to solve meaningful problems and continue growing as both a developer and a teammate.
